# Bitneedle Patent Notice and Interoperability Pledge

Certain Bitneedle technologies are patent pending, including technologies for
encoding compressed audio into a visually renderable, record-like image with an
Archimedean spiral data path, and for related picture-record creation,
authoring, distribution, commercialization, and marketing workflows.

This repository is published to make Bitneedle records inspectable and
self-sufficient for artists and users. The source code license is the Apache
License, Version 2.0, in `LICENSE`. Apache-2.0 includes the patent license
stated in that license for the covered Work. This notice explains Wavey's
intent for Bitneedle patent rights outside that covered Work.

## Limited decoder pledge

Subject to Apache-2.0 and the limits below, Wavey will not assert its Bitneedle
patent claims against a person or project solely for:

- implementing, using, modifying, or distributing open source software that
  decodes, verifies, validates, indexes, previews, plays back, audits,
  preserves, or extracts data from Bitneedle picture-record objects;
- using Bitneedle format information to determine whether a Bitneedle object is
  authentic, self-contained, playable, recoverable, or correctly formed; or
- preserving, migrating, or privately backing up lawfully obtained Bitneedle
  objects for playback, archival access, accessibility, or security research.

This pledge is intended to let artists, collectors, listeners, archivists,
marketplaces, and independent developers know that Bitneedle objects can remain
inspectable and playable without relying on Wavey's hosted services.
